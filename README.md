<p align="center">

---

## 🌟 About the Project

**Alumni Bridge** is a college-focused web platform designed to connect current students with experienced alumni.

Students can discover alumni based on their desired career roles and expertise, ask career-related questions, and request mentorship. Alumni can share their professional experience, provide guidance, answer student queries, and support the next generation of students.

The goal is simple:

> **Connect experience with ambition.**

---

## 🎯 Problem

Students often have questions such as:

- What skills should I learn for my desired career?
- How do I prepare for placements?
- Which technologies are important in my field?
- How can I get guidance from someone already working in the industry?
- What career path should I choose?

At the same time, alumni have valuable professional experience but often lack a centralized platform to share their knowledge with current students.

### 💡 Alumni Bridge brings both sides together.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 👨‍🎓 For Students

- 🔐 Student login interface
- 🔎 Search alumni by desired role
- 👤 View alumni profiles
- 💼 Explore professional expertise
- 💬 Ask career-related questions
- 🤝 Request one-on-one mentorship
- 📚 Get guidance about skills and higher studies

### 🎓 For Alumni

- 🔐 Alumni login interface
- 👤 Create professional profiles
- 💼 Add current role and company
- 🧠 Showcase areas of expertise
- ✍️ Share career guidance
- 💬 Answer student questions
- 🤝 Mentor current students

---

## 🔍 Alumni Discovery

Students can search for alumni based on their desired career path.

Example roles include:

- Software Engineer
- Data Scientist
- Product Manager
- Web Developer
- AI/ML Engineer
- Data Analyst

This helps students find people whose professional experience aligns with their career goals.

---

## 🤝 Mentorship

Alumni Bridge focuses on more than just profile discovery.

Students can request mentorship from alumni and seek guidance related to:

- Career planning
- Technical skills
- Placements
- Higher studies
- Industry expectations
- Projects
- Professional growth
